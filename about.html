What is Knowzilla?

KnowZilla Trivia Game is a versatile platform designed for education, entertainment, skill development, professional assessments, and engagement. It helps users reinforce learning, prepare for exams, and conduct self-assessments across various topics.

"Knowzilla" is a creative name combining "Know" (representing knowledge) and "zilla" (a suffix often used to indicate something big or powerful). The name suggests a platform that provides powerful and expansive knowledge, possibly through quizzes, educational content, or informative activities.
